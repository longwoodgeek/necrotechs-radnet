# NecroTechs engineering charter

> We resurrect dead technology and transcribe obsolete formats.

**Maximum curiosity. Measured power.** If lives are on the line, maximum effort must remain accountable to safety and evidence.

- **Preservation first:** Preserve source captures, raw logs, identities, and working media before changes. Publish reviewed summaries rather than sensitive raw artifacts.
- **Measured over imagined:** Distinguish exploratory successful observations from controlled performance measurements. Missing metrics stay missing.
- **Transport does not equal endorsement:** Network access need not imply agreement with another participant; community anti-harassment policy still applies.
- **Human responsibility:** AI can assist research, documentation and code, but field operations, safety, licensing, attribution and releases require human verification.
- **Open and repairable:** Accessibility, interoperability, limited budgets and long-term maintenance are core design constraints.
- **No unauthorized control:** Remote management and emergency activation require explicit authorization, auditability, scoped roles and safe defaults.

## AI collaboration disclosure

These public documents were prepared with AI assistance using a human operator's firsthand reports and private test records. AI assistance is disclosed rather than hidden. Experimental software is never presented as hardware-validated without an actual test. Respectful disagreement about AI use is welcome; harassment is not.
